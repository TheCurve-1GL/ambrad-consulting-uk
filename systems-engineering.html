The problem

Questions

Common questions

What is model-based systems engineering (MBSE)?

MBSE describes a system with one connected model instead of a pile of separate documents. Requirements, functions, interfaces and assets all link together, so a change in one place shows its effect everywhere else. SysML v2 is the current modelling language for it.

Do we need to buy MBSE software first?

No. Most failed MBSE programmes started with a tool. Our readiness review looks at people, skills and model ownership first, and only then at which tool fits.

How does systems engineering fit with asset management?

ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288 describes how a system is engineered across its life, and ISO 55001 describes how assets are managed for value. They cover the same assets from two angles. We help the two teams use one set of information instead of two.

Which sectors do you work in?

Highways and transport, rail, aviation, water and energy, and nuclear and major programmes. The common thread is owners and operators running complex networks of assets.
