The problem

Good teams, tired processes

Operations and maintenance teams are usually full of capable people working around processes that nobody owns. Procedures sit in binders, reports take days because three teams each hold a piece, and the same faults come back every winter.

The cost rarely shows up as one big number. It hides in overtime, repeat visits, late reports and audit findings. We make it visible, in hours and cost, so you can decide what to fix first.

Questions

Common questions

What does operational excellence mean for an infrastructure operator?

It means running and maintaining the network in a way that's safe, reliable and good value, and getting a little better every year. In practice that's clear processes, named owners, the right measures and a habit of fixing root causes instead of symptoms.

Do Lean and Six Sigma work for asset-heavy organisations?

Yes, if they're sized properly. We use the parts that suit maintenance and operations work, such as process mapping, root cause analysis and simple measures, and leave out the heavy statistics unless the problem calls for them.

Will this help with ISO 9001 and ISO 55001 audits?

It should. Owned processes and clear measures are exactly what auditors look for. We tie the work to your quality and asset management systems rather than building something separate.

How long does a diagnostic take?

It depends on the size of the operation and how many teams are involved. We agree the scope and timescale after the first conversation, and you see the evidence before you commit to anything more.

How do we start?

Book a 30-minute conversation and bring the process that causes you the most pain. We'll tell you whether it's worth a closer look.
