What could move your numbers up or down

Every organisation is different. These six things make the biggest difference, and the four-week plan checks each one.

AI you already use

The example assumes little AI in use today. If your teams already use it well, some of the savings are already banked.

Software you already pay for

Copilot or AI built into your existing systems may already cover some tasks. We count each saving once.

How settled your processes are

Clear, repeatable processes are quick to improve. Messy or undocumented ones need sorting out first.

The state of your data

AI works best on data that's in one place and trusted. Scattered or poor-quality data slows things down.

People and culture

Freed time is only real if people use the tools. Training and trust matter as much as the technology.

Leadership and rules

Security, regulation and your own governance can limit where AI is allowed. Clear sponsorship from the top speeds everything up.

How we sort the work

Every task goes into one of four types

The same four types drive the estimator above and the plan you get at the end.

1

Keep it human

Judgement, relationships and decisions that need a person. AI stays out.

2

AI drafts, a person checks

AI does the first pass and a person reviews it and owns the result.

3

AI handles it

Routine, rules-based work that AI runs, with a person picking up the exceptions.

4

New with AI

Work you couldn't afford to do before, plus the oversight that keeps AI honest.

How the estimate works

Nothing hidden. Check our sums.

  1. Hours a year = people × productive hours (1,650 unless you change it).

  2. Hours freed = hours × (Type 2 share × 25% + Type 3 share × 70%), then minus 15% for the oversight AI needs.

  3. Cost of an hour = salary × (1 + 30% on-costs) ÷ productive hours.

  4. Value of staff time freed = hours freed × cost of an hour.

  5. Lower headcount = value of staff time freed × the share you expect to take as fewer posts. The rest is people reassigned to higher-value work.

  6. Added value = rework avoided (2.5% of Type 2 and 3 time) + new work at £45 an hour.

  7. Net value a year = staff time freed + added value + software cancelled − AI running costs. Risk is shown on its own.

  8. All figures are full potential. Plan on about a quarter in year one, as teams adopt each change. Monthly figures are the annual estimate divided by 12. Real savings and incidents won't arrive evenly month by month.

These are cautious starting assumptions, not industry benchmarks. Results vary with how much AI you already use, the software you already pay for, how settled your processes are, the state of your data, your people and your rules. The plan swaps every one of them for your own figures.
