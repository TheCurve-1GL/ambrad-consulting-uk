Questions

Common questions

What is PFI handback?

At the end of a PFI or PPP contract, the assets go back to the public sector client in the condition the contract requires. If they fall short, the contractor can face deductions, retentions or claims. Starting the review years before expiry gives time to close the gap at a sensible cost.

What is a deductions exposure review?

A look at your performance and availability regime to find where deductions are likely and what they would cost. It turns a list of risks into a number the board can act on.

Do you work for clients or contractors?

Both. We've worked on the private side, the public side and both at once, so we understand what each side needs from the evidence.

Can you help us get ready for ISO 55001?

Yes. We assess your asset management maturity against ISO 55001 and help close the gaps. We're not a certification body, so we can be candid about where you stand.
