Questions

Common questions

What is the AI Plan?

A short, practical document that lists every regular task in a team, sorts each one into keep it human, AI drafts and a person checks, or AI handles it, and prices the time and cost involved. It tells you what to automate first and what to leave alone.

Which AI tools do you use?

Whichever ones your organisation approves. We start with the tools you already pay for, and only suggest new ones where the plan shows a clear return.

Is our data safe?

We check your AI and data policies before we build anything, and your data stays in the tools you approve. Where a task involves sensitive data, the plan says so and the design reflects it.

Will AI replace our staff?

That isn't the aim. The aim is to take copying, chasing and routine admin off skilled people so they can do the work that needs them. Some work should never be automated, and the plan says which.

How do we start?

Book a 30-minute conversation and tell us which team spends the most time on admin. We'll tell you whether an AI Plan is worth it.
